A Good Partnership

Clear Expectations. Better Outcomes.

A strong audit works best when both sides know the boundaries and responsibilities from day one.

  • What is the typical timeframe for an HR Compliance Audit?
    A standard audit typically takes between 1 to 3 weeks depending on organization headcount, number of operating locations, and the agreed scope of records to be sampled.
  • How do you protect sensitive employee & payroll data?
    All engagements are governed by strict confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements. Evidence is reviewed via secure protocols with restricted access, and personnel data is handled with complete discretion.
  • How are findings prioritized in the final report?
    Observations are categorized into High, Medium, and Low priority based on statutory liability, regulatory exposure, and business operational risk. Each finding is paired with a clear, realistic corrective action.
  • What happens after the audit is delivered?
    We conduct an executive leadership readout to walk through the observations. If Implementation Support is in scope, we assist your team in updating policies and tracking corrective action completion.

Important Notice: This consultancy review supports compliance readiness; it is not a guarantee of legal compliance or a substitute for advice from a qualified legal or statutory professional where required.
