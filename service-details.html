The 6 Core Audit Modules (Selectable & Modular)

MODULE 01

HR Statutory & Compliance Audit

Comprehensive review of your adherence to applicable central, state, and local labor legislation. We verify mandatory registers, filings, licenses, and statutory notices to eliminate vulnerability to labour inspections and financial penalties.

Key Review Items:
  • Shops & Establishments Registration & Renewals
  • Statutory Registers (Muster Roll, Overtime, Fines, Deductions)
  • Minimum Wages, Payment of Wages, & Bonus compliances
  • Mandatory notices display & annual returns filing proof
Deliverables Included:

Statutory Compliance Gap Matrix, missing register inventory, penalty risk evaluation, and template register formats for instant regularization.

MODULE 02

HR Operations & Process Audit

Diagnostic evaluation of the employee lifecycle from recruitment and onboarding to performance, promotion, grievances, and exit separation. Identifies operational bottlenecks, inconsistencies, and administrative vulnerabilities.

Key Review Items:
  • Onboarding workflow, verification, & induction standards
  • Probation evaluation, confirmation, & extension workflows
  • Attendance, biometric/remote leave tracking & approval loops
  • Offboarding: resignation, notice periods, handovers, clearance & F&F
Deliverables Included:

HR Process Map & Bottleneck Analysis, standard operating checklist (SOP) gaps, turnaround-time (TAT) benchmarks, and handover protocol templates.

MODULE 03

Policy, POSH & Employee Handbook Review

Comprehensive review of company policies, employee handbook, and sexual harassment prevention framework. Ensures all documents are up-to-date, legally robust, culturally aligned, and clearly understood by the workforce.

Key Review Items:
  • POSH Internal Committee (IC) constitution & annual report filing
  • Code of Conduct, anti-harassment, & whistleblower mechanisms
  • Leave policy (earned, sick, casual, maternity, paternity) statutory alignment
  • IT usage, data protection, confidentiality & remote working rules
Deliverables Included:

Policy Gap & Redline Analysis, Employee Handbook amendment recommendations, and POSH readiness assessment including mandatory workshop schedules.

MODULE 04

Employee Records & Employment Contracts Audit

In-depth sampling of physical and digital personnel files, employment contracts, offer letters, NDAs, and confidentiality agreements to identify gaps in legal enforceability and privacy protection.

Key Review Items:
  • Enforceability of employment agreements, probation & termination clauses
  • Non-disclosure, non-compete, IP assignment & confidentiality covenants
  • KYC compliance, educational verification records & background checks
  • Secure personnel documentation storage & digital access controls
Deliverables Included:

Personnel File Sampling Audit Report, contract risk exposure register, revised legally vetted offer letter/agreement templates, and KYC checklist.

MODULE 05

Compensation, Benefits & Payroll Compliance Audit

Comprehensive reconciliation of payroll registers, wage structures, minimum wage adherence, statutory benefits (PF, ESIC, Gratuity, Professional Tax), overtime payments, and leave encashment calculations.

Key Review Items:
  • Minimum wage rate compliance across categories (unskilled, skilled, managerial)
  • PF & ESI wage ceiling deductions, calculation method & timely deposits
  • Overtime calculation formulae and statutory limits observance
  • Gratuity liability provisioning, actuarial requirements & payout accuracy
Deliverables Included:

Payroll Reconciliation Audit Sheet, statutory exposure financial estimate, wage restructuring blueprint, and compliant payslip template.

MODULE 06

Remediation Action Plan & Implementation Advisory

An audit is only as valuable as the transformation it drives. We provide an execution-ready remediation roadmap with prioritised milestones, accountable owners, drafting support, and post-audit advisory check-ins.

Key Review Items:
  • 30-60-90 day remediation roadmap sorted by risk severity
  • Turnkey document drafting (letters, circulars, registers, policies)
  • Leadership debrief presentation & management summary
  • 60-day post-audit compliance validation check-in
Deliverables Included:

Master Remediation Action Register (Excel + PDF), leadership slide deck, standard templates kit, and ongoing quarterly advisory support.

Structured Execution

Our Proven 6-Stage Audit Methodology

STAGE 01
Discovery & Scoping Call

Align on company headcount, entity structure, multiple locations, specific pain points, and target scope.

STAGE 02
Secure Information Request

Issuance of a tailored Information Request Checklist and secure folder setup with strict NDA protections.

STAGE 03
Field Review & Sampling

Rigorous verification across sampled personnel files, payroll sheets, challans, registers, and POSH records.

STAGE 04
Gap Analysis & Risk Scoring

Classification of observations into High, Medium, and Low risk tiers based on regulatory exposure.

STAGE 05
Report & Executive Debrief

Comprehensive Audit Report delivery and interactive leadership debrief session with actionable insights.

STAGE 06
Remediation Support & Follow-Up

Assistance in redrafting, setting up missing registers, closing compliance gaps, and quarterly check-ins.
