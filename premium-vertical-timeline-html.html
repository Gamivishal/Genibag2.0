Our Method

From Discovery to a Practical Action Plan.

A structured 6-stage engagement designed to minimise disruption and maximise clarity.

Stage 01

DISCOVER

Understand business context, workforce profile, operating locations, and primary leadership concerns.

Stage 02

COLLECT

Request and gather relevant personnel records, policies, statutory registers, and process evidence.

Stage 03

REVIEW

Test data samples, compare written documents to actual daily practice, and identify gaps or inconsistencies.

Stage 04

PRIORITISE

Classify observations by regulatory impact, business urgency, and practical effort required to resolve.

Stage 05

REPORT

Present evidence-led observations, executive summaries, risk registers, and recommended corrective actions.

Stage 06

SUPPORT

Agree on owners and realistic timelines; provide hands-on follow-up guidance where in agreed scope.
