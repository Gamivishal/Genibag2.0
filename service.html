Modular Offerings

One Partner. A Clearer HR Picture.

Tailored to your organisation’s size, sector, and operating realities.

01

HR Compliance Review

Review applicable labour-law requirements, statutory records, registers, notices, returns, and filing workflows.

  • Statutory registers & display notices
  • Annual returns & statutory filings
  • Contractor / vendor documentation
02

HR Process Audit

Examine the full lifecycle: recruitment, onboarding, attendance, leave, employee changes, and exit clearance.

  • Joining, induction & confirmation flows
  • Biometric attendance & leave balances
  • Resignation, clearance & Full & Final
03

Policy & Documentation

Assess HR policies, appointment letters, employee handbook consistency, and mandatory POSH compliance.

  • Controlled employee handbook versions
  • POSH policy, ICC committee & awareness
  • Grievance & disciplinary guidelines
04

Employee Records Audit

Check completeness, accuracy, accessibility, confidentiality, and statutory retention of personnel files.

  • Offer, appointment & promotion records
  • KYC, qualification proofs & background
  • Secure file access & retention policy
05

Compensation & Benefits

Review salary structures, internal alignment, statutory wage baselines, and payroll reconciliation controls.

  • Minimum wage & statutory salary alignments
  • Overtime, shift & allowance records
  • Payroll approval matrix & reconciliation
06

Action Plan & Advisory

Prioritise findings by impact, assign responsible owners, and support implementation of agreed actions.

  • Formal Findings Register & Risk Rating
  • Corrective Action Tracker with deadlines
  • Executive readout & remediation support
