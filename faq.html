Practical HR Governance For Your Workplace
Have a question about your workforce? Speak to an HR Auditor
  • What is the typical timeframe for an HR Compliance Audit?
    A standard audit typically takes between 1 to 3 weeks depending on organization headcount, number of operating locations, and the agreed scope of records to be sampled. A clear timeline and milestones are established during our initial discovery call before review begins.
  • How do you protect sensitive employee & payroll data?
    All engagements are strictly governed by bilateral non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). Data is inspected via secure, restricted-access protocols. We operate on a representative sampling methodology, meaning sensitive information is reviewed discreetly and never stored or shared outside the audit team.
  • How are findings and non-compliances prioritized in the final report?
    Observations are categorized into High, Medium, and Low risk based on statutory exposure, regulatory penalties, and operational disruption. Each finding is paired with an actionable, realistic corrective action and suggested owner so leadership can immediately address vulnerabilities.
  • What specific HR operational areas and records are examined?
    Our audit covers five core modules: Statutory Compliance & Registrations, Hiring & Onboarding Documentation, Employee Master Files & Lifecycle Records, Compensation & Wage Slips, and Core Workplace Policies (including POSH, Leave, Attendance, and Code of Conduct).
  • Does Geniebag provide formal legal counsel or statutory representation?
    Geniebag Solutions provides HR advisory and audit services. Our reports and recommendations do not constitute formal legal counsel. Where formal statutory representation, legal defense, or litigation advice is required, we collaborate directly alongside your legal advisors.
  • What happens after the audit findings are delivered?
    We conduct an executive readout session with your leadership team to walk through observations and address questions. If Implementation Support (Tier 2 or Tier 3) is selected, our team assists you in updating policy drafts, standardizing registers, and tracking corrective actions.
