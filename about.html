Our Method

From Discovery to a Practical Action Plan

A structured 6-stage engagement designed to minimise disruption and maximise clarity.

01

DISCOVER

Understand business context, workforce profile, locations, and key leadership concerns.

02

COLLECT

Request relevant records, policies, statutory registers, and process evidence.

03

REVIEW

Test data samples, compare documents to daily process, and identify gaps or inconsistencies.

04

PRIORITISE

Classify observations by regulatory impact, operational urgency, and effort to resolve.

05

REPORT

Present evidence-led observations, executive summaries, and recommended corrective actions.

06

SUPPORT

Agree on owners and timelines; provide follow-up remediation guidance where in scope.
