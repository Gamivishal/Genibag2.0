Krishant

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Good HR governance isn’t about generating thick binders of paperwork that sit on a shelf. It is about creating transparent, proportionate, and review-ready systems that protect your business, earn employee trust, and empower leadership with clarity.

As the Founder and CEO of Geniebag Solutions (The Management Basket), Krishant spearheads the firm’s mission to provide practical, evidence-led HR compliance, audit, and advisory services. With an in-depth background across labour compliance architectures, workforce governance, and operational HR risk mitigation, he works closely with promoters, corporate boards, and CXOs to convert ambiguous statutory regulations into disciplined daily workflows.

Under his leadership, Geniebag Solutions has established a distinctive audit methodology that prioritises actionable findings over bureaucratic noise—ensuring organisations achieve statutory readiness, safeguard enterprise continuity, and cultivate an equitable workplace culture.

Our Philosophy

Practical, Proportionate and People-Aware.

Four principles shape every engagement — from the first scoping conversation to the final leadership readout.

No one-size-fits-all checklist. Applicable requirements depend on establishment type, headcount, operating location, and current law — so scope is confirmed at kickoff.

01

Practical HR Governance

Controls proportionate to your actual workforce, locations and ways of working — built to be used, not filed away.

02

Transparent Processes

Agreed scope, sampling and boundaries from kickoff, so leadership always knows what is reviewed and why.

03

Employee-Centric Practices

Fair, understandable processes for policies, communication, records and employee concerns — handled with discretion.

04

Risk-Based Decision Making

Observations classified by regulatory impact, operational urgency and effort to resolve, so actions are prioritised rather than overwhelming.
