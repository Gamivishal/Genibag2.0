# Geniebag Solutions - HR Consultancy & Compliance Audit

Official website for **Geniebag Solutions / Genie Bag HR Consultancy** (*"The Management Basket"*).

> **"Clarity for your people. Confidence for your business."**

---

## 📌 Services & Capabilities
- **HR Compliance Review**: Labour-law statutory registers, returns, filings, and notices.
- **HR Process Audit**: End-to-end employee lifecycle from onboarding to separation.
- **Policy & Documentation Assessment**: Employee handbook, appointment letters, POSH policies, and governance.
- **Employee Records Audit**: File completeness, KYC, personnel records, and retention controls.
- **Compensation & Benefits Review**: Payroll verification, statutory wage compliance, and allowances.
- **Action Plan & Advisory**: Evidence-led findings register, risk prioritization, and corrective action tracking.
